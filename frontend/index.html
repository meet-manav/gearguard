Predictive Maintenance & Engineering Diagnostics

ISO vibration severity guidelines, failure mode signatures, and step-by-step Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs).

Healthy Baseline Operation

Vibration Signature: Low amplitude broadband noise (< 2.5 g RMS), absence of prominent GMF sidebands.

  • Maintain lubricant top-off every 500 operating hours.
  • Inspect radial lip seals for thermal degradation.
  • Archive monthly FFT baseline snapshots for historical trending.

Bearing Raceway Wear

Vibration Signature: High frequency acoustic excitation (3–5 kHz), elevated kurtosis (> 3.5).

  • Measure bearing cap temperatures with infrared pyrometer (< 75°C normal).
  • Perform grease solvent test to identify microscopic metal shavings.
  • Flush bearing housing and repack with NLGI-2 synthetic grease.

Shaft & Coupling Misalignment

Vibration Signature: Dominant 1X and 2X rotational speed peaks; phase difference across coupling > 90°.

  • Check motor feet for soft-foot gap using a 0.05 mm feeler gauge.
  • Inspect elastomeric coupling spider for shear tears or hardening.
  • Re-align motor-to-gearbox shafts using dial indicator or laser tool.

Broken Gear Tooth (Critical)

Vibration Signature: Severe repetitive transient shock pulses at Gear Mesh Frequency (GMF) with sidebands.

  • Halt motor immediately; apply Lockout/Tagout (LOTO) disconnect.
  • Drain oil through 100-micron strainer to recover tooth fragments.
  • Replace pinion and wheel gear pair; verify backlash with lead wire.
